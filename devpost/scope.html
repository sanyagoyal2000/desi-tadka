Reel to Real

Paste a Western fitness reel and get a written, visual card translated for Indian kitchens and Indian ingredients, so you never have to rewatch the video.

The unique kernel

A cultural translator for fitness content. Western influencers' recipes and protein advice fit their bodies, diets, and grocery stores. This turns their reels into something people in India can actually use — Indian ingredients, written down and pictured.

Who it's for

The person

People in India who scroll Instagram fitness reels regularly. You're the first user (and vegetarian), but the target is India broadly.

Today

Saves reels, can't relate to the protein advice or ingredients, never cooks the recipes, and has no time to rewatch workouts.

Core loop

See a reel → paste it → get a card → cook it or do it. Comes back every time a reel catches your eye.

Why it matters to you

"I am a vegetarian so most of the times I am not able to relate with protein requirements and most recipes… I can't spend time watching videos again."

Inspiration

Gamma and Napkin for the messy-input-to-polished-visual moment. Strava and Cult.fit for the fitness-app feel.

Proof-of-concept boundary

Explicitly cut
