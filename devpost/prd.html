Behaviors & how you'll know they work

StarRecipe swaps
  • Tapping a swap icon shows at least one Indian-ingredient suggestion
  • Typing a custom replacement swaps that ingredient
  • "Make it vegetarian" replaces every non-vegetarian ingredient
  • Swapped ingredients are visibly highlighted; others aren't
  • Protein and calories change after a swap that affects them
Paste a reel
  • Recipe reel link → recipe card with the original recipe
  • Workout reel link → workout card with the original plan
  • Not a valid link → "Wrong link" before any processing
  • Valid but inaccessible → "Unable to access, please check"
Recipe card

Dish name, ingredients, number of steps, steps, protein, calories, prep time, picture.

  • All fields appear for a recipe reel
  • Original shows a reel frame; after a swap, an AI-generated picture of the new version
Workout card & swaps

Workout name, exercises, reps, rounds, frequency, images. Same swap icon pattern (assumption). Reel frames for originals, AI images for swapped-in exercises.

  • All fields appear for a workout reel
  • Swapping an exercise replaces every occurrence in the plan
  • A 4th swap is blocked with a short message
SaveSave & Home list
  • After Save, the card appears on Home
  • Saved cards are still there after reopening
  • Tapping a saved card shows the final version with swaps applied
